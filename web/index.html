    How SignSpeak works

    1. Frame yourself. Stand waist-up with both shoulders visible. The Body badge on the video turns green when you're tracked.
    2. Sign one word at a time. Hold the sign until the bar under the caption fills, then lower your hands. The word joins the transcript.
    3. Pause to speak. Stop signing for a moment and the words become a sentence that is spoken aloud. Or press Speak now.

    Fix mistakes in the transcript: drag words to reorder, press the cross to remove one, or click a sign in Vocabulary to add it.
